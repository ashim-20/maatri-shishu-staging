# design.md · Maatri-Shishu

## Voice
Simple, warm, empathetic, and trustworthy. Short sentences. Human-centered language. Avoid technical jargon.

## Color
Primary #166B55 · Accent #E6B85C
Background #FAFAF6 · Text #18332D
Secondary #EAF2ED · Muted #718078

## Type
Headings: Poppins Bold
Body: Inter
Use clear hierarchy and readable font sizes.

## Slide rules
16:9 · 6 slides · 5 minutes total · max 25 words per slide
One central idea per slide.
Use authentic Nepalese maternal healthcare and community health imagery.
Prefer real app screenshots, local health workers, mothers, children, and health-post settings.
Keep layouts spacious and uncluttered.
Use diagrams only when they explain a real workflow.

## Story
Human problem → Existing challenges → Maatri-Shishu solution and demo → Social impact and sustainability → Built with AI → Vision and ask.

## Never
Invented statistics · unsupported medical claims · generic stock photos · technical jargon · overcrowded slides · more than 2 fonts · fabricated testimonials · unverified vaccination recommendations.
